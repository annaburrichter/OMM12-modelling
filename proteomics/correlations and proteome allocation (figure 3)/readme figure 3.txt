input: excel files reanalysed_single_vs_community, reanalysed_mouse_vs_invitro, annotation file protein_annotation.tsv

reanalysis_correlation: generates correlation coefficients per species for all comparisons (Figure 3a and Extended Data Figure 6) for all detected proteins

correlation_cat_G_only: generates correlation coefficients per species for all comparisons for all proteins assigned to COG category G (Figure 3b)

mass_fraction_calculation: generates mass fractions for single COG categories and COG category groups for both the single vs. community and the in vitro vs. in vivo dataset (Figure 3c, d and Supplementary Figures 9, 10 and 11). Plotting and statistics were performed in GraphPad Prism.
